Travel Log — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 May 2026 · Last updated: 21 May 2026

Short version. Travel Log builds a journal of your trips using location and photo data on your device. The developer does not receive, store, or process any of your data. Nothing is sent to any server controlled by us. If you turn on iCloud sync, your data syncs through your own iCloud account (Apple is the processor under Apple's iCloud terms; we cannot see it). There is no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDK, no tracking of any kind.

1. Who publishes this app?

Konstantinos Fragkoulis (referred to in this policy as "we", "us", or "the developer"), the independent publisher of the Travel Log iOS application. You can reach the developer at travellog@kfragkoulis.com.

Because the app stores and processes data only on your device and (optionally) in your own iCloud account, the developer is not — in operational terms — a controller or processor of your personal data: there is no server, no database, and no analytics pipeline on our side. This policy nevertheless describes the in-app data handling so that you can make an informed decision before granting permissions.

2. What data does Travel Log handle on your device?

  • Approximate location. Travel Log registers for iOS Significant Location Change events — background "you have moved a meaningful distance" pings at roughly 500 m / cell-tower resolution. It does not record continuous GPS. Each event becomes a "ping" stored locally; pings are clustered into "stays" and "stays" into "trips."
  • Photo metadata, only for photos you have authorised the app to read. When you opt into the Photo Library Import feature, or when you browse a trip's photos, the app reads each photo's creation date and GPS coordinates from its EXIF metadata to match it to a trip. The app does not upload, copy, or modify your photos.
  • Reverse-geocoded place names. Coordinates are translated into human-readable city / country names using Apple's MapKit service. Your device sends the request directly to Apple; we do not see the request or the response. Apple's privacy policy applies to that lookup.
  • AI-generated trip titles. The app uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework to produce a concise title for each trip. All inference runs on your device. Prompts and outputs never leave the device.
  • Your "Home" and "Routine places." Coordinates and radii that you enter in onboarding or Settings, so the app knows which places to exclude from your trip list.

3. What data does the developer collect?

None. The app contains no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, and no third-party network call. The developer has no infrastructure that receives any data from your installation of the app. We do not know that you have installed it; we do not know how you use it.

4. Where does the data live?

  • On your device, in the app's private SwiftData store (sandboxed, accessible only to Travel Log).
  • If you enable iCloud sync in Settings, the same data syncs through your own iCloud account to your other Apple devices. The container is a CloudKit private database — by Apple's design, only you (and the devices signed into your Apple ID) can read it. The developer cannot. See Apple's iCloud privacy policy for how Apple itself handles iCloud data.

5. Third parties

None. There are no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no third-party data processors. The only external services the app talks to are Apple's own: MapKit (reverse geocoding), PhotoKit (your photo library), CloudKit (your iCloud), and Apple Push Notification Service (so that CloudKit can notify your other devices of changes).

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). It does not knowingly process personal data of children. If you believe a child has used the app, you can simply delete the app and (if iCloud sync was used) clear its data from iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

7. How long is data kept?

Indefinitely on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual trips or stays from inside the app.
  • Delete all data from Settings → "Delete All Data" (this preserves only your Home and Routine Place definitions, so you do not have to set them up again).
  • Delete only the photo-import data from Settings → "Delete imported data" — cleanly removes everything that was created by the photo-library backfill.
  • Delete the app from your device, which removes the local store. If iCloud sync was on, the data in iCloud remains until you also remove it from your iCloud storage (iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage → Travel Log → Delete Data).

8. Your rights

Because the developer holds none of your data, almost every data-subject request is satisfied by the in-app deletion tools above. The following rights are nevertheless acknowledged:

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland (GDPR & UK GDPR)

Under Articles 15–22 of the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because all such data resides on your device or in your iCloud account, you can exercise all of these rights directly via the app's data-management features and your device's iCloud controls. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data-protection supervisory authority.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know, delete, correct, and limit the use of their personal information, and to opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell or share any personal information. The "know" and "delete" rights are satisfied by the in-app data-management features.

For any rights request that the in-app controls do not satisfy, please write to travellog@kfragkoulis.com.

9. International users

Because no data ever reaches the developer, no international transfer to the developer takes place. iCloud sync routes through Apple's infrastructure under Apple's iCloud terms; that transfer is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

10. Security

On-device data is stored in the app's sandboxed file container, protected by iOS Data Protection (encrypted when the device is locked). iCloud-synced data is protected by Apple's iCloud security model (end-to-end encrypted for users with Advanced Data Protection enabled). The developer holds no keys, no credentials, and no copies.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of the page will change. Material changes (for example, introducing any actual data collection by the developer) would also be surfaced inside the app before they take effect.

12. Contact

For any privacy-related question, please write to travellog@kfragkoulis.com. We will reply within a reasonable time. If your request concerns your statutory rights under GDPR or CCPA, please include enough context for the request type to be identified.
